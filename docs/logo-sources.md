The Tsinghua source has a wide white canvas. Its square CSS viewport removes only lateral blank space and preserves the complete emblem and surrounding clearance; the downloaded file is unchanged. Other images use `object-fit: contain`. No logos are recoloured, stretched, or redrawn.

The University of Melbourne remains a text entry because its [official personal-website FAQ](https://brandhub.unimelb.edu.au/resources/governance/advice-for-students) says its logo is not available for personal use.

The assets remain the property of their respective institutions, and this repository does not grant a logo licence. See also [SJTU's usage policy](https://vi.sjtu.edu.cn/index.php/articles/bulletin/16) and the linked university identity pages for use conditions.
